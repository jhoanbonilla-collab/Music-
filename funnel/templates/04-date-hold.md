# 04 · Date hold — 48 hours, no payment

**Trigger:** client replies **HOLD** (or asks for time to decide).
**Do first:** block the date in the calendar as `HOLD — [CLIENT]`, set the hold expiry
in the tracker (**now + 48 hrs**), move card to **Date Hold**.
**Rule:** one active hold per date — first HOLD wins.

---

## A · Hold confirmation (send immediately)

**Subject:** [EVENT DATE] is on hold for you — until [HOLD EXPIRY DAY, DATE, TIME]

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Done — **[EVENT DATE] is now blocked in the calendar under your name.** No payment,
no obligation.

The hold runs for **48 hours, until [HOLD EXPIRY — e.g. Thursday, March 12, 6:00 PM]**.
Until then, nobody else can book the date.

To confirm, just reply **BOOK** any time before then — the contract and deposit link
come straight over, and the date is officially yours.

If the 48 hours pass without word, the date quietly reopens — no hard feelings, and
you're welcome back if it's still free.

**XolosaX**
[PHONE / WHATSAPP]
