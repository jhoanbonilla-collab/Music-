# 09 · Post-gig — thank you + review / referral ask

**Trigger:** event completed. **Send within 48 hours** while the glow is fresh.
**Rule:** one primary ask per message — the **review**. The referral rides along as a
P.S. Move card to **Completed**; log review + referral outcome in the tracker.

---

## Email (next morning is the sweet spot)

**Subject:** What a night, [CLIENT NAME] 🎷

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Still buzzing from last night. [ONE SPECIFIC MOMENT — e.g. "When the whole room sang
the hook during the second party set — that's the stuff this job is made of."] Thank
you for having me be part of it.

**One small favor** — a quick review helps other people planning a [EVENT TYPE] find
this and know what to expect. Two minutes, means the world:

**→ [REVIEW LINK — Google / The Knot / etc.]**

*(If you'd rather record a 20-second video saying what you told me at the end of the
night — even better. Just reply with it.)*

I'll send over a couple of the photos/clips I grabbed as soon as they're cleaned up —
feel free to post them, tag **[@HANDLE]**.

Thanks again for the trust. Until the next one,

**XolosaX**

**P.S. — the referral part:** if a friend, planner, or colleague is planning an event,
send them my way ([FORM LINK] or just this email). When their booking confirms, you
both get **[REFERRAL CREDIT — e.g. 10% off a future booking / $100 credit]**. Great
events travel by word of mouth — you're the mouth. 😄
