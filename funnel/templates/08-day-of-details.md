# 08 · Day-of details — send the day before (or morning of)

**Trigger:** scheduled reminder at event − 1 day.
**Channel:** WhatsApp / SMS — short, warm, zero questions. CC the day-of coordinator.

---

## To the client (day before, early evening)

> Hi [CLIENT NAME] — tomorrow's the day 🎷 Quick confirmation so you don't have to
> think about me at all:
>
> **Arriving [ARRIVAL TIME]** at [VENUE], set up and sound-checked well before
> [FIRST SET TIME]. Your requests are arranged and ready.
>
> My direct line: **[PHONE]** — I've also got [COORDINATOR NAME]'s number and will
> coordinate with them, not you.
>
> Nothing needed from your side. See you tomorrow — it's going to be great.

## To the coordinator (same time)

> Hi [COORDINATOR NAME], this is XolosaX — the saxophonist for [CLIENT]'s [EVENT TYPE]
> tomorrow at [VENUE]. Arriving **[ARRIVAL TIME]** for setup; performing
> [SET SCHEDULE]. I need [SPACE NEED] + one power outlet — all confirmed with [CLIENT],
> just flagging so you have it. Anything shifts in the runsheet tomorrow, I'm on
> **[PHONE]**. See you there!

## Morning-of ping (optional, travel-day gigs)

> Morning [COORDINATOR NAME] — XolosaX here, on schedule for [ARRIVAL TIME] at [VENUE].
> Any last-minute changes, this number. 🎷
