# 01 · Auto-response — instant

**Trigger:** inquiry form submitted (email auto-reply), or first DM / referral contact.
**Goal:** confirm received, set the reply-time expectation, keep momentum.

---

**Subject:** Got it — your XolosaX inquiry is in 🎷

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Thanks for reaching out — your inquiry for **[EVENT DATE]** just landed and the date is
being checked against the calendar right now.

**You'll get a personal reply within 24 hours** with availability and a tailored quote.

While you wait: [LINK — performance videos / Instagram] for a feel of what your guests
will experience.

Talk soon,
**XolosaX**
[PHONE / WHATSAPP] · [INSTAGRAM] · [WEBSITE]

## DM variant (Instagram / TikTok / WhatsApp — send manually or via saved reply)

> Hey [NAME]! Thanks for the message 🎷 So it doesn't get lost in DMs, drop your event
> details here — takes 2 minutes: **[FORM LINK]**
> The moment it's in, you'll get availability + a quote within 24 hours.

> Hi [NAME] — [REFERRER] passed along your details, love that. To get you an exact
