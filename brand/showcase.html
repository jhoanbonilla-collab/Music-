The Symbol

The Golden S — sax monogram & diamond seal

An elegant monoline S — the saxophone's own S-curve — its lower bowl opening into an abstracted bell, with a row of three key pearls running down the body. Enclosed in a fine diamond seal whose diagonals and four points form the X of XolosaX, it becomes a crest. The music is hidden in the geometry — a refined cue, never a literal horn. Use the seal for badges and stamps; the open S for screens and small sizes.
