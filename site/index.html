The Symbol

The Golden S

An elegant monoline S — the saxophone's own S-curve — its lower bowl opening into an abstracted bell, with three key pearls running down the body. Enclosed in a fine diamond seal whose four points trace the X of XolosaX, it becomes a crest. The music is hidden in the geometry — a refined cue, never a literal horn.
